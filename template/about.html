About FemAgro Farms

Nearly five decades of passion, innovation, and sustainable farming excellence.

Our History

Founded in 2006, Built for the Future

FemAgro Farms was born from a simple belief: farming and technology are not opposites — they are partners. Founded by the FemAgro family in 2006, we began as a small cooperative serving local Nigeria farming communities.

Today, we serve thousands of farms across 30+ countries, providing precision technology, soil science, and sustainable practices that help farmers grow more with less — for the planet and for generations to come.

Our Mission

Nurturing Growth, Sustaining the Future

Our mission is to revolutionize agriculture through innovative technology while preserving the deep-rooted traditions that make farming one of humanity's most vital pursuits.

Founded in 2006, we started with a simple observation: the gap between agricultural research and the farmer in the field is enormous. Millions of farmers make decisions every season without access to the knowledge that could double their yields and protect their livelihoods.

We bridge that gap. Our platform translates rigorous agronomic science into guides, articles, and tools that any farmer can use — whether they're growing on half an acre or five hundred.
